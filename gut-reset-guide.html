The Gut Reset
Starter Guide

Where to start when everything feels off

by Izabela Ostatek

Your gut doesn't break overnight. By the time symptoms show up, the damage is layered. This guide cuts through the noise — what to remove first, what to add, and in what order.

01

What your gut is actually telling you

Most people treat the symptom. Bloating → antacids. Fatigue → caffeine. Skin flares → creams. The root cause stays untouched.

Gut dysfunction doesn't always mean gut pain. It shows up as brain fog, disrupted sleep, low mood, weight that won't shift, skin that reacts to everything. The gut is involved in more than digestion.

The question isn't "what's the symptom?" It's "what's driving it?"

02

5 signs your gut needs a reset

If three or more of these sound familiar, your gut is involved.

  • 1You feel worse after eating — even food you consider "clean"
  • 2Energy crashes after lunch regardless of what you ate
  • 3Bloating that doesn't fully resolve by evening
  • 4Brain fog that comes and goes with no clear external trigger
  • 5Skin reacting — acne, eczema, redness — with nothing obvious to blame
03

Start here: what to remove first

Before adding anything, remove these for 21 days. Most people feel a difference within 10.

Gluten

Not because it's a trend. It creates microscopic inflammation in the gut lining — especially in a gut that's already compromised. Two weeks off it will tell you whether it's relevant for you.

Conventional dairy

The proteins — casein and whey — are among the most common gut irritants. Hard aged cheeses and ghee are usually tolerated. Milk, soft cheese, and yoghurt are where most people react.

Processed seed oils

Sunflower, rapeseed, vegetable oil. These drive inflammation quietly. Switch to olive oil, coconut oil, or butter. The impact on bloating and energy is often immediate.

04

The morning protocol

Do this before coffee, before food, every morning for 7 days.

  1. 500ml warm water + lemon Drink it slowly. Not cold, not hot. Room temperature to warm.
  2. Wait 20 minutes This is the step most people skip. It matters. The lemon water activates digestive enzymes before food arrives.
  3. Protein and fat first — not carbohydrates Eggs, smoked salmon, avocado, nuts. Not toast. Not oats. Stabilising blood sugar in the morning changes how you feel for the rest of the day.

Most people notice a shift in bloating within 5 days. If you don't, the issue is deeper — which is where lab testing and a tailored protocol come in.

05

What most people get wrong

They add supplements before removing the problem.

Probiotics in an inflamed gut have limited impact. Collagen in the morning doesn't counteract what you're eating at lunch. Supplements are the rebuild phase — not the starting point.

The order is: remove → repair → rebuild. Jumping to step three without doing steps one and two is why nothing seems to work.
